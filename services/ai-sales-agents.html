Questions

Before you ask…

Will it make things up?

It only answers from the knowledge and catalogue we give it, and it’s built to fail closed: if it isn’t sure, it hands over to a human instead of guessing.

Which languages can it handle?

English and Swahili out of the box, and most major languages. It can match how your customers actually write, including mixed Kenyan English.

What does it cost to run?

Usually a few dollars to a few tens of dollars a month in AI usage for an SME, plus WhatsApp costs. I’ll estimate it from your real message volume before we start.

Will customers know it’s AI?

That’s your call, and I recommend being upfront. Customers mostly care that they got a fast, correct answer.
