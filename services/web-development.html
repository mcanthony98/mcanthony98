Quick answers

How much does a website cost?

From KES 35,000 for a one-page site, KES 100,000 for a full business website, and from KES 150,000 for a website with custom features, AI automations and integrations. You get one fixed price before we start.

How long does it take?

1 to 2 days for a one-page site and about 1 week for a business website. Custom builds depend on the features you need.

Are there any hidden costs?

No. There are no other costs. Your website is live and maintained for one full year. After that, it’s KES 10,000 a year to renew your domain, hosting and maintenance.

Can I update it myself?

Yes. Every site comes with an admin portal, and I show you how to use it.

Is the domain and email in my name?

Yes. Your domain, hosting, email and data always belong to you.
