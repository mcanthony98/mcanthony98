You probably don’t have a traffic problem. You have a leak.

  • Your agency stops at traffic. Your sales team starts at the enquiry. Nobody owns the middle.
  • I measure every stage, find the one losing the most money, and fix that first.

How I work

One loop. Every client. Every month.

Trying to optimise everything at once is how retainers quietly turn into busywork. So every month I find the single stage failing hardest, fix that one thing, and prove whether it moved.

  1. Pull

    Real numbers from GA4, Search Console, ads, CRM and the actual sales record.

  2. Diagnose

    Name one failing stage: demand, visibility, conversion, follow-up or retention.

  3. Prioritise

    Rank the three cheapest fixes by expected revenue, not by what’s fun to build.

  4. Ship

    Build it and put it live. Tracking, page, automation, integration.

  5. Report

    One page: what changed, what moved, what it was worth, what’s next.

  6. Close the loop

    Check against banked money, because analytics and revenue aren’t the same number.
